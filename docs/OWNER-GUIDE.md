# Connecting your LinkedIn account (5 minutes)

You've been asked to connect your LinkedIn account to our research tool. You will log in to LinkedIn once, inside a secure browser window that we host. You don't install anything.

**Your password stays with you.** You type it directly into LinkedIn's own login page. We never see it and never store it. You can disconnect at any time (see the end of this guide).

## What you need

- A laptop or desktop with Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. A phone screen is too small.
- Your LinkedIn email and password.
- Your phone, in case LinkedIn asks for a code or an approval.
- The **link** and **password** we sent you. The link is long; copy all of it.

## Steps

1. **Open the link** we sent you in your browser.
2. Your browser asks for a username and password:
   - Username: `owner`
   - Password: the one we sent you (it is *not* your LinkedIn password)
3. You see a window with a **LinkedIn login page** inside your browser page. It is a browser running on our server.
   - If you are asked to click **Connect**, click it.
   - If the screen is black for a moment, wait about 10 seconds.
4. Click into the **Email or phone** box and type your LinkedIn email. Click the **Password** box and type your LinkedIn password. Click **Sign in**.
5. If LinkedIn asks you to verify, do what it says:
   - Enter the code sent to your email or phone, **or**
   - Approve the sign-in in the LinkedIn app on your phone, **or**
   - Complete the "security check" puzzle.
6. Wait until you see your **LinkedIn home feed**. The window may close on its own after a few seconds. That means it worked.
7. Close the browser tab. **You're done.** Tell the person who sent you the link.

The link works for 30 minutes and only once. If it stops working, ask for a new one.

## Good to know

- You can keep using LinkedIn on your phone and laptop as usual.
- LinkedIn may show a new "signed in" location under *Settings → Sign in & security → Where you're signed in*. That entry is our tool.
- Please **don't** click "Sign out of all sessions" unless you want to disconnect. If you do, we'll send you a new link.
- If LinkedIn ever warns you about unusual activity, tell us straight away and we'll pause the tool.

## Sales Navigator accounts

If we asked you to connect **Sales Navigator**, the steps are the same, with two differences:

- The link ends in `/vnc.html`. Click **Connect** after entering the page password.
- After signing in, click **Sales Navigator** (or go to `linkedin.com/sales`) once, so it opens. Then close the tab. Leave the browser window there as it is; the tool keeps using it.

## Disconnecting

Tell us, and we'll delete the stored session. You can also remove it yourself: LinkedIn → *Settings → Sign in & security → Where you're signed in* → sign out the session you don't recognise as your own device.
